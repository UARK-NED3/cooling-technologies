# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Approach temperature | Temperature difference between two streams or between a controlled object and its relevant sink at a specified location. The definition must state the locations used. |
| Coefficient of performance (COP) | Ratio of useful cooling duty to electrical or work input over a stated system boundary. |
| Cooling load | Rate at which heat must be removed to meet a stated thermal requirement. It may include internal generation and environmental gains. |
| Heat flux | Heat-transfer rate per area, usually `W/m²`. The relevant area must be stated. |
| Heat sink | A body, fluid, or environment that receives rejected heat. A heat sink has a finite temperature and capacity. |
| Latent heat | Energy transferred during a phase change at a stated pressure, without a temperature change of the pure substance during the phase change. |
| Sensible heat | Energy transfer associated with a temperature change without phase change. |
| Temperature lift | Difference between the controlled temperature level and the heat-rejection temperature level. The exact definition depends on system locations. |
| Thermal resistance | Ratio `ΔT/Q̇`, with units `K/W`, under the specified conditions. |
| Transient | A condition in which temperatures, stored energy, flow, or loads vary with time. |
