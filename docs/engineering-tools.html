--- layout: default title: Engineering screening tools --- # Engineering screening tools These browser-based tools support transparent first-pass calculations. They do not transmit or save your inputs. Every result is **derived, screening-level evidence**: use actual fluid properties, geometry, operating state, pressure drop, controls, safety requirements, and validation before design or procurement. All inputs use SI units. Record the system boundary, source, units, and evidence status of each input in the [assumption and evidence register](templates/assumption-evidence-register.md).

Allowable thermal resistance

Rth,max = (Tlimit − Tsink) / Q̇

Resistance-network temperature rise

ΔT = Q̇ Rth

Single-phase liquid-flow target

ṁ = Q̇ / (cp ΔTfluid)

Sensible thermal-storage mass

m = E / (cp ΔT)

Declared-boundary COP

COPR = Q̇cooling / Ẇinput

## Interpretation and limits - A thermal-resistance result represents the stated source-to-sink path; it does not establish a junction, interface, local-fluid, or component-case temperature unless those locations and resistances are explicitly in the model. - The liquid-flow calculation gives an average energy-balance target. It does not determine pump head, local convection, branch distribution, pressure drop, cavitation margin, material compatibility, or component temperature. - The storage calculation assumes all stored energy reaches a uniform thermal mass over the stated temperature change. It ignores rate limits, packaging, heat loss, and recharge. - The COP calculation is comparable only when cooling duty, electrical input, source/sink state, part-load condition, and time basis match.
